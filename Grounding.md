#stefansolo 
Grounding is an action in partner dance exemplified by increasing ones association to the floor inversely relative to the partner.  

This makes it a [[First Partner]] principle.  A [[Relaxation]] of [[Pillar of Frame|Frame]].  The opposite of and an antithesis of [[Seek]].

Grounding involves lowering ones [[Center of Weight]].  Channeling momentum held in the body into the ground.  As well as relaxing connection to the partner if association to the partner is greater then association with the ground.
