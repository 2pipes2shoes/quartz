#stefansolo 
This combination of words refers to a whole category of moves including [[Dips]
and [[lifts]].  These are moves that are generally unsafe when done with people unfamiliar with them.  They therefore require communication and consent to achieve.  
They often involve one or more parties supporting each others weight especially in ways that are not easy to recover solo.   Thus the initiation must be communicated as well as the recovery.
