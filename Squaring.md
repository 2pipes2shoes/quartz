#fordevelopment 
Squaring is a term referring to a combination off [[offset]] and shoulder rotation.
It refers to the goal of facing the targeted part of your partner's body squarely rather then at an angle.  This is important to [[Alignment|align]] the feet and hips, build association and proprioceptive awareness of the partner's body.  There is some variation between dance styles  on this topic and thus we will cover fusion's default and then hint to some variations.
