A ghost step is a type of lead that hides its initiation.  These are similar to [[Force Leading|Force Leads]] but use a more advanced understanding of the disparity between [[Parallel Motion Lead|Parallel Motion Leads]],[[Change of Frame Lead|Change of Frame Leads]] to create initiations that seem to come from no where.  

These are a more advanced type of initiation that often simply results in force leads among the inexperienced.  A proper ghost step requires  a strong grasp of both types of lead as well as [[validation]], [[proprioception]], [[Timing]] and usually [[Rotational Mechanics]].

The fundamental idea is that with both an danced lead and follower you can initiate [[Parallel Motion Lead|Parallel Motion Leads]] over a small amount of space.  This maximizes the amount of time for [[Change of Frame Lead]] and [[Validation]] over the play out of the momentum generated.  

With practice this can turn into a small [[Weight Shift]] without a step combined with a [[Change of Frame Lead]] creating a large motion for the follower or even perpetual follow motion.
