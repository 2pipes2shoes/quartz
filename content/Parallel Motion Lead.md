Parallel Motion Leads (PML) are one of two forms of initiation from which all dance is built the other being [[Change of Frame Lead]].  PMLs are characterized by both parties centers moving in the same direction at the same time.  This allows natural addition of energy from the ground into the follower.  PMLds are often far more still and subtle then CoFLs but can add energy into the partnership and move the frame as a whole.  This creates a feeling of subtle strength.
