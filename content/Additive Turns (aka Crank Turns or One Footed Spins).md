Crank turns are a rotational method where the initiator adds energy to the already rotating follower.  In infinite turn theory this is only needed to speed up a turn.  In finite turn theory this is needed to a small degree when doing perpetual turns.  

Crank Turns have a negative association as they are often done with [[Force Leading|Force Lead]] technique or an inaccurate understanding of the follows rotational axis leading to hard engagement during unequal motion.

Crank turns are useful for [[Single Axis Turns]] as otherwise the follower would need to [[Row]]
to maintain perpetual energy.
