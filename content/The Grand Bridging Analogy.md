The Grand Bridging analogy is a way to parse difference of perspective.  This was invented for Neurodivergence but also has application across the board including Partner Dance, Politics, Communication, as well as perspective broadening.

The fundamental analogy works like this:
1. Everyone is Different.  Each person has a barrier to connect/communicate with anyone else.
2.  We describe this barrier as the ocean on a vast globe. Each person has their own island of comfort where they naturally connect.  
3. Some Islands are closer to others.
4. If you know where someone is comfortable you can build a bridge for your island towards theirs.  This takes energy as it moves away from your defaults.
5. Building a bridge half way is doable.  Building a bridge from your island 100% of the way is quite hard.
6. If you notice someone building a bridge to you its easy to do you half of the work bridging to them.  That way you meet in the middle and don't have to build a bridge all the way back down to them.
