#inprogress 

Compression based frame is one of the two ways to increase association in frame with a partner, the other being [[Leverage Based Frames|Leverage Based Frame]].  Compression based frames are created by a [[Connection Points|Connection Point]] that has investment towards the partner's center.  This creates a mutualistic frame that trends towards an invested shared center.  This is due to the more quickly required base movement to change the frame or the shape of the frame.
