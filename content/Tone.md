#fordevelopment 

Tone is a muscular engagement in the body.  It is characterized with only a single muscle of a matching pair in the body being engaged.  This allows connection to the partner who engages a single muscle as well to counteract creating association and balance.  [[Tension]] is a similar principle but includes both paired muscles being engaged at least partially in a single person.

We aim for using tone in partner dance over tension due to the fact that tension uses additional muscular engagement and creates mixed signals/less data for [[proprioception]].  This does not mean Tension has no uses see [[Tension]].
