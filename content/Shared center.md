Shared Center is a model to help understand connection.   It is the balance point between you and your partner.  As a model that point does not have to be occupied to exist.  It is the most static point in [[Change of Frame Lead|Change of Frame Leads]] and the central point that needs to move when communicating [[Parallel Motion Lead|Parallel Motion Leads]].   It is also the point of orbit of a partnership.    As a balance point it is located on the line between the [[Center of Weight]] of a partnership.  It is usually balanced but adjusts closer to a partner of larger mass or who is taking an axis in.
