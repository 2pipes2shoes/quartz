Posture is the arrangement of the body in relationship to the ground/[[First Partner]].
While relaxed on the ground there is no muscular engagement to create posture.
While standing you must engage some musculature to support each [[Joint]] to support it and prevent a sudden return to first partner.  These muscles are held loosely engaged using [[Tone]] and, as they are resisting gravity, no [[Tension]] is needed.   In a specific posture certain engagement and thus resulting mechanical defaults result.  This creates a starting off point for [[proprioception]] and [[Pillar of Frame|frame]].

To keep Frame and posture distinct.  Think of posture as the internal structure of the body focusing on your relationship with the floor and modeling for a posture.  Frame overlaps thinking of the build of the association between your center and your partner center.  This utilizes the top part of posture.
