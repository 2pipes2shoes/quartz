Fusion does not have a default posture when viewed as a framework.  Instead we will be covering a crossroads.  A place to find your partner when you lose them.  Among familiar dancers they may never reach this posture if they have a basis of dances far from this crossroads.  Fusion dances based in the three historic parent dances: West Coast Swing, Argentine Tango and Lindy.
This crossroads however shows up in martial arts as well as sports and thus it may be close to a universal crossroads.

#### Elements from the ground up
- weight over ball of foot
	- Slightly inside edge
- ankles bent heels barely off the ground if at all
- knees bent
- hip rotator bent
- spine strait and angled forward
- head stacked
- arms relaxed and at sides
