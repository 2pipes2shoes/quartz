The Speed of Connection is the rate, closest to real time with lag approaching zero, at which a lead is incorporated by the follow.  This baseline reaction time within the partnership differs from dancer to dancer.  Circumstances and states can also have an affect on reaction times.  For example, dancer reaction times may be affected by quality and quantity of sleep, hydration, food, substances, emotional states, etc.

The Speed of Connection needs to be recalibrated with every new dance partnership.  The Speed of Connection can be calibrated by starting out in slow motion with your partner matching you in real time, and gradually increasing your speed until the moment you notice your partner lagging behind.  The Speed of Connection is lost once your partner can no longer match you in real time, at which point lag is inherently created.
