Bops are type of [[Weight sharing]] that uses a fulcrum point and inertia to temporarily lift someone from the ground.  Due to the location of the fulcrum related to the imitation angle.  These usually only lift the followers center of weight a few inches and do not hold it for more then a few seconds.
These are often used as an entry point to other more principled and complex weight sharing moves.
