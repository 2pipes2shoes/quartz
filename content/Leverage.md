Leverage is a lean away from your partner's center.  This builds association in a physical, still relaxed way which increases [[Proprioception]].  Leverage is often used in playful dances as it does less to restrict perpendicular motions.  This is due to leverage being self stabilizing due to [[Tether and Tether Length|Tether]] Principles. 

We use leverage instead of other possible terms to be clear that it has no inherent association with muscle engagement.
