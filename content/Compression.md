Compression is a lean towards your partner's center.  This builds association in a physical, [[Proprioception|proprioceptive]], and supportive way which increases [[proprioception]].  Compression is often used in grounded dances as it brings both parties close to the shared center and reduces [[Degrees of Separation]].  This makes a more grounded connection and provides a more direct default connection.

We use Compression instead of other terms as it has no direct association with muscle engagement.
